Austin's Trading DNA:

**Philosophy**
Story of a company beats fundamentals every time. Glance at the balance sheet, know the thesis cold. If a thesis takes more than 30 minutes to construct, it has too many variables. Boring strategies last longest — the goal is scalable, repeatable outcomes, not excitement.

Tech-heavy, high-beta by preference. Young, willing to take risk — buy the dip like a hobby rather than hedge. Avoid high-dividend-yield stocks unless incidental; wants companies reinvesting in themselves. Ignore 95% of headlines. Value shows up naturally; don't hunt it.

**Options = <10% of portfolio.** Rest is MAG7 buy-and-holds, VTSAX, TQQQ. The wheel is diversification, not the whole stack. Options trading goes through IBKR; long-term investments stay in Fidelity.

**Wheel / CSP execution**
Default: 30–45 DTE, 0.20–0.30 delta. Compress delta when IV is elevated — at ~70 IV, sell closer to 0.20; at ~40 IV, can go up to 0.30. Sweet spot is ~40 IV; will stretch to 50–65 occasionally. Target ~0.5% weekly premium, not 1%+ (too volatile for his taste).

Entry timing: prefer oversold setups — RSI oversold + price near lower Bollinger Band coming back toward mid. Favor sideways or red action for CSP entry. Avoid names that already made a big move up. Scout on 1D chart, enter/exit on 5M (4H for shorter-dated positions). Never sell a CSP on Friday — sell Monday instead for nearly the same value.

BTC rules: 30% captured → close Monday; 50% → close Tuesday; 70% → close Wednesday; let it ride Thursday/Friday. GTC orders at 30–50% max profit.

**Regime filters**
SPY below EMA threshold → reduce or pause new long-delta exposure. VIX above threshold → scale down or step aside. Sit on hands when market breadth is bad — this is the biggest edge.

**Assignment & covered calls**
Assignment is by design — only sell puts on names comfortable holding for months. Transition to covered calls. Never sell a CC below cost basis. Roll shorts at 21 DTE or when short delta hits 0.50, whichever comes first — roll same strike, next week, for a net credit.

**LEAPS / PMCC**
LEAPS expire 1.5 years out for time value efficiency and LTCG eligibility (taxable account). In Roth: roll strike up frequently, keep leverage. Short call in a PMCC is a tiny hedge, not a standalone trade — designed to keep assignment risk low while running the LEAP toward LTCG, not to maximize premium.

**What makes him pass**
Overhyped symbol with inflated IV and poor fundamentals. Friday CSP entry. Name that just ripped up. ADR >4% (too volatile). Position sizing: <10% capital per single name.
